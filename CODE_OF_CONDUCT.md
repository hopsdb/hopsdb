# Community conduct

HopsDB welcomes contributors and users of different backgrounds and experience
levels. Be respectful, constructive, and patient in issues, reviews, and other
project spaces. English and Chinese are both welcome.

- Discuss code and ideas without personal attacks or harassment.
- Do not post discriminatory, threatening, or sexually inappropriate content.
- Respect people's privacy. Never publish someone else's credentials or private
  information without permission.
- Accept constructive feedback and make room for others to participate.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or limit
participation to protect the community. Reports involving private information or
harassment should use a private contact channel published by a maintainer, not a
public issue. If no channel is available, request one without disclosing private
details. Maintainers should explain moderation decisions when doing so is safe
and practical.
